# Marketing & Sales

*Ownership: Claude (per DEC-0002). Starts blank — no prior ChatGPT-side marketing work was shared into this repository.*

## Purpose

Positioning, messaging, website copy, pricing pages, SEO, sales scripts, outreach, social, go-to-market strategy.

## Current Priorities

Public marketing site (landing + pricing + live demo) targeted at SMBs (DEC-0026), currently in build.

## Current Decisions

- **DEC-0026** — Target market is SMBs, not enterprise. Public marketing site ships before the client console.
- **DEC-0027** — Site includes a live grounded demo (real Receptionist, seeded demo tenant), a labeled non-live role-picker preview for the other 7 roles, a sales chat widget, and an honest industries section (no fabricated client counts/logos/testimonials). Voice/telephony and general workflow-assistant capabilities are roadmap, not built.

## Approved Work

Two mockups approved as final visual direction and copy (do not redesign): `aether-ai-landing.html` (homepage) and `aether-ai-pricing.html` (pricing page). Design system: navy ink (#101B33), paper cream (#F4F1E6), brass (#A9843C), stamp red (#B5402F); Fraunces (headlines), IBM Plex Mono (labels/data), Inter (body). "Personnel file" concept — AI employees framed as staff (ID cards, status pills, roster), not chatbot/SaaS iconography.

## Constraints

- Core value prop (from Company Bible): "Businesses don't buy AI. Businesses hire employees." Messaging should reinforce hiring/staffing framing over generic AI-tool framing.
- Target market is SMBs (DEC-0026): copy speaks to an owner of a clinic/shop/small firm, never to an enterprise IT/procurement buyer. No SOC2/SSO/SLA claims — the product doesn't have that story yet.
- No fabricated client counts, logos, or testimonials (DEC-0027) — real numbers only once they exist.
