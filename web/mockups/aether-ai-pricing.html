QUESTIONS

Before you hire

What happens when it doesn't know something?

It says so, and hands off — to your team on Essentials, or to Aether on Managed and Dedicated. It never invents an answer about your business.

Can I change roles later?

Yes — add, pause, or upgrade any Digital Employee's tier at any time from their personnel file.

Is my data shared across accounts?

No. Every account's data is fenced off at the database level — nothing crosses between clients.

How fast can I go live?

Most single-role setups are live the same day — you're mainly filling in what the employee should know.

Do I need an IT team to set this up?

No. If you can fill out a form describing your hours, pricing, and policies, you can hire a Digital Employee — no developer or IT department needed.

Is this only for big companies?

The opposite — Aether is built for small and medium businesses that need front-desk coverage but don't have (or can't yet afford) a full team for it.
